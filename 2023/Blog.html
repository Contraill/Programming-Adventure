My Adventure Beggins

Hello friends, In this blog, I want to share with you how I started coding and what I experienced in this process. Coding means learning how to program a computer and how to solve various problems. This is both an interesting and a challenging process.

To start coding, I learned a programming language called Python. With Python, I can do many different things. This makes me very happy.

While learning coding, I also faced many difficulties. I struggled to understand some topics, I dealt with some errors, I got stuck on some projects. But these did not discourage me, on the contrary, they made me more determined.

Since I started coding, a lot has changed in my life. Coding gave me a new perspective, a new passion, a new skill, a new goal. Coding became a way of life for me.

I wish success to those who want to start coding, those who have started and those who continue. Keep following this blog, because I will share more things about coding. See you later!
